Sage & Sand

A boutique yoga studio for slow living.

Book Your First Class

In the quiet of a sunlit room, we unroll our mats and arrive — fully here, fully now.
Sage & Sand is not a gym. It is a place to return to yourself, one breath at a time.

Our Practices

Vinyasa Flow

Link breath to movement in a mindful, moderate-paced sequence. Build strength and grace.

All Levels

Yin Yoga

Long, passive holds that invite deep release into connective tissue and quiet the mind.

Restorative

Restorative

Breathwork

Guided pranayama and conscious breathing to calm the mind and energize the body.

Class Schedule

Room: Sunlit Studio B

Prerequisites: None — all levels welcome

Notes: Bring a towel if you tend to sweat. Mats and props provided.

Room: Garden View Room

Prerequisites: None — suitable for beginners

Notes: Blankets and bolsters provided. Wear comfortable layers.

Room: Quiet Room A

Prerequisites: None — restorative for all bodies

Notes: Eye pillows and weighted blankets available. Stay as long as you need.

Room: Meditation Loft

Prerequisites: None — sit or lie down as able

Notes: No experience necessary. Journaling optional afterward.

Room: Sunlit Studio A

Prerequisites: Some experience recommended

Notes: Moderate pace. Bring water and an open mind.

Your First Class Is Free

No commitment, no pressure. Just show up, breathe, and see what this space feels like for you.
